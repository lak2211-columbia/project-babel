AI Guidelines for Business Ver 1.0 Compiled

April 19, 2024

Joint Press Release with the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications

Aiming to address the recent rapid changes in technology, including the dissemination of generative AI, the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) integrated and updated the existing related guidelines and compiled the AI Guidelines for Business Ver 1.0 based on the results of repeated discussions with experts.

Three guidelines were integrated: AI R&D Guidelines (2017; MIC), AI Utilization Guidelines (2019; MIC), and Governance Guidelines for Implementation of AI Principles Ver. 1.1 (2022; METI).

1. Overview

The AI Strategy Council compiled a Tentative Summary of AI Issues on May 26, 2023, which explained Japan’s need to discuss revision of the existing guidelines in view of the recent dissemination of generative AI.

In response, METI and MIC integrated and updated the existing related guidelines and proceeded with their discussions on well-organized and user-friendly guidelines that target a broad range of AI business operators.

For this purpose, METI established the Expert Group on AI Guidelines for Business and MIC established the Conference toward AI Network Society, and these bodies held a series of meetings respectively.

Based on the discussion results in these bodies, METI and MIC compiled the Draft AI Guidelines for Business and opened a call for public comments on this draft from January 20 to February 19, 2024.

Following this, on April 19, 2024, the ministries reported before the AI Strategy Council the Draft AI Guidelines for Business Ver. 1.0, a compilation based on the public comments and the results of discussions at the joint meeting held on March 14, 2024. The ministries finally compiled the AI Guidelines for Business Ver. 1.0.

Going forward, they will continue to update the guidelines with necessary information.
