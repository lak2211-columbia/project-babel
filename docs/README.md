## Research

- [`research/codebook.md`](research/codebook.md) — the analytical vocabulary. Definitions for every label in use:
  - Binding-strength categories (`binding_law`, `executive_action`, `regulatory_guidance`, `standards_guideline`, `voluntary_commitment`, `strategy_statement`)
  - Safety requirement families (risk assessment, pre-deployment testing, red-teaming, incident reporting, transparency disclosure, model documentation, third-party audit, access controls, compute governance, content provenance, human oversight, registration/licensing)
  - Enforcement mechanisms (civil penalty, criminal penalty, market access restriction, procurement condition, licensing requirement, regulatory supervision, reporting obligation, soft enforcement)
  - Risk framing families (existential/catastrophic, national security, economic competitiveness, fundamental rights, social stability, innovation enablement, consumer/public safety, labor transition)
  - Framing rules, confidence guidance, evidence-span requirements.
