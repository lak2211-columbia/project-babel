# Research Codebook

## Principle

The observatory needs a stable analytical vocabulary. Labels should be broad enough to compare across jurisdictions but specific enough to support meaningful divergence measurement.

## Binding strength

- `binding_law`: enacted law or regulation with legal force
- `executive_action`: executive order, presidential directive, cabinet order, or equivalent
- `regulatory_guidance`: guidance from a regulator with interpretive or supervisory force
- `standards_guideline`: standards body or technical guidance without direct legal force
- `voluntary_commitment`: pledge, code of conduct, or cooperative commitment
- `strategy_statement`: strategic or agenda-setting document

## Framing rules

- Assign one dominant frame when the document has a clearly primary rationale.
- Secondary frames are allowed when they materially shape the text.
- Classification should rely on the document's stated reasoning, not the analyst's external theory.
- Save evidence spans whenever a framing label is assigned.

## Confidence guidance

- High confidence: text gives direct and repeated support for the label.
- Medium confidence: text supports the label but with ambiguity or limited repetition.
- Low confidence: label depends on inference, summary language, or sparse evidence.
